ARCHR Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026  |  Effective Date: Upon Publication

Overview

The Asheville Regional Coalition for Home Repair ("ARCHR," "we," "us," or "our") operates a platform for coordinating disaster-related home repair assistance among a coalition of nonprofit organizations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

We are committed to collecting the minimum amount of information necessary to connect households with repair assistance, and to protecting the privacy of the households and individuals we serve.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to:

  • Applicants and Households who submit information to request home repair assistance
  • Staff and Volunteers of partner organizations who use the platform
  • Partner Organizations that participate in the ARCHR coalition
  • Subcontractors and Vendors who work on repair projects
  • Visitors to our public website and documentation

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact information — name, address, phone number, email address
  • Household information — number of residents, demographic information relevant to eligibility
  • Property information — address, ownership status, type of home, year built
  • Financial information — income documentation for eligibility verification
  • Repair needs — descriptions of damage, photos, and assessment notes
  • Communications — messages, notes, and records of interactions with staff
  • Documents — income verification, identification, contracts, and related files

Information Collected Automatically

  • Session data — login timestamps, IP addresses, and browser information
  • Activity data — records of actions taken within the platform (see Section 5)

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Social Security numbers
  • Bank account or credit card information
  • Immigration status
  • Criminal history
  • Medical records
  • Information about minors beyond what is necessary for household eligibility

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Determine eligibility for home repair assistance programs
  • Coordinate repairs among coalition organizations
  • Communicate with applicants, staff, and partners about case status
  • Track funding and generate reports for grantors and funders
  • Maintain audit trails of all actions taken on a case
  • Improve the platform and the services we provide
  • Comply with legal obligations and funding requirements

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. How We Share Information

Within the ARCHR Coalition

Information about a case may be shared with:

  • Coalition partner organizations that are participating in the repair
  • Staff and volunteers assigned to the case
  • Subcontractors and vendors working on the repair
  • Funders and grantors as required for reporting and compliance

With Service Providers

We use a limited number of third-party services to operate the platform. These include:

  • Dropbox — for secure document storage (see Section 6)
  • Airtable — for data management during the current transition period
  • Email and communication services — for notifications and correspondence

These service providers are contractually required to protect your information and may only use it to provide services to us.

With Legal Authorities

We do not share information with law enforcement or government agencies without a valid legal warrant, court order, or as otherwise required by law. We will notify affected individuals when legally permitted to do so.

In Aggregate Form

We may share aggregated, de-identified information (such as the number of households served or the total funding distributed) for reporting and public communications. This information does not identify any individual or household.

5. Activity Logging and Audit Trail

The platform maintains an immutable activity log that records actions taken within the system. This log includes:

  • Who performed an action
  • When the action occurred
  • What was changed
  • Which case or application was affected

Why we do this:

  • To maintain accountability and transparency
  • To support peer review and quality assurance
  • To satisfy grantor and funder reporting requirements
  • To protect the integrity of the assistance process

The activity log is immutable — entries cannot be altered or deleted once recorded. This protects everyone involved by ensuring a trustworthy record of what occurred.

6. Dropbox Integration

We use Dropbox as a secure document storage provider. This section explains how we use Dropbox and how your information interacts with their services.

What We Store in Dropbox

  • Case documents (applications, assessments, contracts, completion certificates)
  • Photos of properties (before, during, and after repairs)
  • Income and ownership verification documents
  • Financial records (invoices, receipts, quotes)

How We Access Dropbox

Our platform connects to Dropbox using the Dropbox API. Access is limited to:

  • A single, organization-controlled Dropbox account
  • Specific folders dedicated to case documents
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)

What Dropbox Can and Cannot Do

  • Can store files we upload to it
  • Can retrieve files we request from it
  • Cannot access files outside our designated folders
  • Cannot use your information for their own purposes beyond providing storage services
  • Cannot share your information with third parties

Dropbox's Own Privacy Practices

Dropbox maintains its own privacy policy and security practices. We encourage you to review Dropbox's Privacy Policy at https://www.dropbox.com/privacy to understand how they protect data.

We have configured our Dropbox integration to:

  • Use a dedicated, organization-controlled account
  • Store files in encrypted form (at rest and in transit)
  • Restrict access to authorized personnel only
  • Log all access to documents

7. How We Protect Information

We take the security of your information seriously. Our safeguards include:

Safeguard Description
Encryption in transit All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS
Encryption at rest Documents stored in Dropbox are encrypted
Access controls Only authorized staff and partners can access case information
Role-based permissions Different roles have different levels of access
Activity logging All actions are recorded in an immutable audit trail
Limited retention We retain information only as long as necessary (see Section 8)

8. How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as necessary to:

  • Complete the repair project
  • Satisfy grantor and funder reporting requirements (typically 7 years)
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Maintain historical records of assistance provided

After the retention period, we securely delete or anonymize information.

Requestors may request deletion of their information after their case is closed, subject to legal and funder retention requirements.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for future use
  • Know who has accessed your information

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@archr.org.

10. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify active platform users of material changes
  • Post the updated policy on our website

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
