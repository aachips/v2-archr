### Where Does the Money Come From?

Funds can come from federal grants, such as from FEMA and HUD. They can come from state funding, such as North Carolina Disaster Relief. We have private foundation funders like Cisco and Land of sky. We have partnerships with organizations, non-profits, and businesses. And we also rely on individual donations to sustain our work.

### The $50,000 Per Home Cap

Each eligible household is capped at $50,000 for disaster-related repairs. This is per disaster, per home (if another disaster hits, new cap applies). Funds must be spent on eligible repairs only. The cap applies to total cost across all funding sources

### How We Track Funding Per Case

- **Approved Amount**: What we've committed to spend
- **Incurred Costs**: What we've actually spent (invoices, materials, labor)
- **Reimbursed Amount**: What has been paid out
- **Remaining Balance**: What's left to spend
- **On Hold**: Money set aside for pending items

### The Three Key Financial Dashboards

|Dashboard|Purpose|User|
|---|---|---|
|**Past Week**|Monitor recent activity|All staff|
|**All Time**|Track historical performance|All staff|
|**Organization Specific**|Per-org metrics|Admin, Bursar|

### The Metrics We Track

**Past Week:**

- Total Applications
- Assessments Assigned & Completed
- Average Triage Score
- Total Individuals Served (from household size)
- Income, County, Issue Type Breakdowns

**All Time:**

- Total Jobs
- Jobs In Progress / Completed / Withdrawn
- Average Job Cost
- Income & County Breakdowns 
- Highest & Lowest Job Costs

**Organization Specific:**

- Total Jobs per org
- Jobs In Progress / Completed / Suggested
- Assessments Assigned / Completed / Pending
- Cost Extremes and Averages

### Reimbursement Workflow



- Subcontractor submits invoice → enters queue
- Bursar reviews (checks against scope, budget)
- Approve or reject with reason
- If approved → payment issued
- Track aging: goal < 30 days

### Preventing Double-Dipping

- Case locking prevents two orgs from claiming same case
- Duplicate address detection
- Overlapping invoice alerts
- Every payment has audit trail
