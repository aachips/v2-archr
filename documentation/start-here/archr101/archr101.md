## Meet Eileen Bailey

Eileen Bailey is a home-owner in Swannanoa. The Baileys have lived in Buncombe County for twenty-five years. Before Hurricane Helene, the Bailey household was a space of memories and event hosting for family holidays. While the Baileys never had a ton of money, they had everything else.

Then Hurricane Helene happened. Many of the Baileys' neighbors' homes were completely devastated beyond repair. The Bailey household had a tree fall on it. No one was hurt. But there was catastrophic damage to the living room. A tree literally fell through it! The rest of the house was undamaged, but the flooding and opening created by the impact made the home unlivable and unprotected from the elements.

Eileen heard about ARCHR's Home Repair Program and submitted an application. In the aftermath of the storm, there was no electricity, water, or internet—sometimes for weeks, some places for months. So after the first opportunity the Baileys had to escape and charge their phones, they returned to take pictures of the damage and begin their intake application with ARCHR.

ARCHR has been operating since 2021, and when the storm hit, we already had the intake and management infrastructure to start running. This is why we were successful: because a plan was already in place for when disaster hit.

The Baileys could complete their intake application piecewise, saving their progress along the way. They could work on the application offline and submit it once they could go to their local public library, which had Wi-Fi internet up and running. When they finished and submitted the intake, a partnership of non-profits and aid workers was ready to receive it and mobilize.

Upon submission, a series of events was triggered. Several organizations committed to doing home repairs were alerted. Each had different eligibility criteria for taking on the repair. 

Because the Baileys were at 80% of the Average Median Income, and because Joe Bailey, a disabled textile worker, resides there, they were eligible for repair assistance with Disability Works, a local agency specializing in families with disabled members. That agency agreed before a storm happened that they would mobilize resources, staff, and volunteers after a disaster, towards home repair assistance. A dozen other organizations signed onto this pact before a disaster hit.

## What This Platform Does

ARCHR connects people who need home repairs with people who can do them. The platform handles case management, document storage, financial tracking, and communication.

**The full journey:** application → eligibility → assessment → planning → work → completion

## 2. The Six Phases of a Repair Project

| Phase | What Happens | In Eileen's Story |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| **0 - Submission & Pre-Screening** | Application submitted; initial checks run | Eileen completes intake at the library |
| **1 - Intake** | Case created; assessor assigned; documents collected | ARCHR creates Eileen's case record |
| **2 - Processing** | Assessment performed; scope defined; budget set | An assessor visits the Bailey home |
| **3 - Planning** | Contract created; resources scheduled | Disability Works plans the repair |
| **4 - Project Execution** | Work performed; progress tracked | Crew repairs the Bailey home |
| **5 - Completion & Report** | Final walkthrough; certificate signed; costs reconciled | Eileen signs off on the completed work |

## 4. The Four Big Systems

### Tasks, Logs & Events
Everything that happens gets logged. Every task, every communication, every event. If it happened, it's in the system.

*When Eileen called to ask about her application timeline, that call was logged. When the assessor visited, that visit was logged. When the crew finished the roof, that completion was logged.*

> **Learn more:** [Tasks, Logs & Events](tasks-logs-events.md)

### Document Bucket
All files are organized by case in a standardized folder structure. Every document has a consistent naming convention.

*The Bailey's intake form, assessment photos, contract, and completion certificate are all stored in their case folder, organized and easy to find.*

> **Learn more:** [Document Storage & Generation](document-storage.md)

### Progress Tracker
An immutable ledger of what happened, when, and by whom. Nothing is ever deleted or overwritten.

*Every step of the Bailey's journey—from application to completion—is recorded forever.*

### Quick Add
One-click logging from the field. Click "+ Add Event," select the task type, enter date and notes, and save.

*The crew lead marked "Roof Repair Complete" from their phone before they even left the Bailey property.*

> **Learn more:** [Quick Add Progress Tasks](quick-add-progress-tasks.md)

## 5. Quick Add Explained

Quick Add is designed for busy people in the field. You don't need to fill out a long form—just the essentials.

**How it works:**

1. Click **"+ Add Event"** from any case
2. Select the **task type** (ASSESS, SCOPE, START, COMPLETE, PROGRESS)
3. Enter the **date** and any **notes**
4. Upload **photos or documents** (optional)
5. Click **Save**

*The assessor visited the Bailey home, took photos, and logged the assessment in under two minutes using Quick Add.*

**What happens after you save:**
- The entry goes to the **Unverified** queue
- A **Task Rabbit** reviews and approves it
- Once approved, it counts toward metrics
- All changes are permanent in the ledger

## 6. What Happens After I Submit Something

| Step | What Happens | Who Does It |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **1. Quick Add** | User logs an event | Any user |
| **2. Unverified Queue** | Entry waits for review | System |
| **3. Task Rabbit Review** | Entry is standardized | Task Rabbit |
| **4. Approval** | Entry becomes official | Task Rabbit |
| **5. Metrics Update** | Counts are updated | System |
| **6. Ledger Entry** | Change is permanent | System |

*Eileen's application went through this exact process. When she submitted it, it entered the queue. A caseworker reviewed it, confirmed eligibility, and moved it forward. Every step was recorded in the ledger.*
