At time of writing in 2026, there is a digital landscape for sharing what we know with people we often don't know. We can share our stories, experiences, and blueprints through the internet. 

We are attempting to share how after a devastating weather and climate event in our region, how we were able to support neighbors helping neighbors in getting their homes fixed. It may not be the perfect blueprint. But it's tested and has worked for us.

Not everything on here is the most intuitive to pick up and follow. We are a work in progress. 

It would be great if you could click a button, and instantly ensure your community is prepared for worst. Life doesn't work that way. This is a scaffolding for you and your people to begin forming and building on working relationships. This is a conversation starter. 

Our public repository for developers is under construction. Our development team wants to collaborate with other localities about disaster preparedness and running a home repair operation.

Right now the best we can do is our Help Library. Our aspiration is open-source and grassroots developed.

---
## So here's our advice

1) Get to know your neighbors, before something terrible happens.
2) Identify what you can offer in times of trouble. 
3) Build a team with your most trusted peoples and start with small actions.

Non-Profits and Institutions can be great. But sometimes all we have is ourselves.
