Our core philosophy is to Assume Eligibility, unless proven otherwise. We assume every applicant is eligible until we find disqualifying information.

In our records of over 1,000 applications, we end up having to refuse about 60 due to not meeting the income requirements. That's .6% of our client base. Potentially hundreds of thousands of dollars in spent administrative labor, to deny a handful of people the help they are desperately seeking and applying for. We want to be able to help everyone who has a disaster home repair need in our region. We are limited by the rules given to us by our funders. Having a diverse source of funding allows us to help more people. This system is not perfect, but we do our best to make it better each day.

#### Eligibility for home repair will always be determined by a human being. It will never be a robot determination.

We may use automations and robots for making certain tasks easier. A computer may generate eligibility flags for each case, to better help us determine which clients are eligible for help from what partner organizations. But a human being will always be in charge of that decision. We wish to preserve the right for you to always be able to reach a human when contacting us. 

Eligibility checking is a process, it's not meant as a gatekeeping moment.

#### Here are the Requirements we look at when reviewing your Application:

|Category|Requirements|
|---|---|
|**Geography**|Property must be in service area (Buncombe, Madison, Henderson, Haywood counties)|
|**Home Ownership**|Applicant must own the home (or have legal authority to authorize repairs)|
|**Primary Residence**|Must be primary residence, not rental or vacation property|
|**Income**|No lower limit. Upper limit varies by organization (typically 80% AMI)|
|**Cause of Damage**|Damage must be related to Hurricane Helene (September 2024)|
|**Household Size**|Counts residents 50%+ of the time|
|**Demographics**|No discrimination; special programs for veterans, elderly, disabled|

### The Eligibility Review Process

- **Automated Check**: System runs initial eligibility on submission
- **Manual Review**: Caseworker verifies documentation. We reach out and make attempts to obtain missing documents.
- **Organization Match**: System finds best-fit organization (income, location, repair type)
- **Final Determination**: Assigned organization confirms eligibility
    
### What Makes Someone Ineligible (Disqualifiers)

- Property outside service area
- Not a homeowner
- Not primary residence
- Previous assistance received for same repair
- Income exceeds all partner maximums
- Mobile home without lot ownership
- Under 18 without guardian consent
    
### What If I'm Denied?

If you are denied/deemed ineligible, you will receive a clear explanation why. You can appeal this denial with additional documentation. If we can, we will gladly refer you to other resources.
