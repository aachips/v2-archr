# Document Management: Uploading, Organizing & Finding Files

> **Who this is for:** Everyone who uploads, views, or manages documents in the platform — which is every role.

## Why This Is Needed

The document system is powerful but complex. Photos prove the work was done. Income docs prove eligibility. Contracts prove agreement. **Every document in a case is evidence** — for the applicant, the organization, and the funder.

A case with no documents is a case that can't be closed properly. A case with disorganized documents is a case that fails an audit.

---

## What Kinds of Documents Exist

| Document Type | Who Uploads | When | Why |
|---------------|------------|------|-----|
| **Income verification** | Applicant, Envoy | During intake | Proves eligibility |
| **Photo ID** | Applicant, Envoy | During intake | Confirms identity |
| **Before photos** | Assessor, Crew | At assessment | Documents damage |
| **During photos** | Crew, Volunteer | During repair | Shows progress |
| **After photos** | Crew, PM | At completion | Proves work done |
| **Contracts** | PM, Admin | Before work starts | Legal agreement |
| **Invoices** | Subcontractor, Bursar | After work completed | Payment request |
| **Assessments** | Assessor | After site visit | Repair scope |
| **Communication logs** | Anyone | After any contact | Record of interaction |
| **Progress notes** | Anyone | As work happens | Timeline evidence |

## Uploading Documents

### Step-by-Step

1. **Open the case** you're uploading to
2. **Navigate to the Documents section**
3. **Click "Upload Document"**
4. **Select the document type** from the dropdown (income verification, before photo, etc.)
5. **Choose the file** from your device
   - Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
   - Max file size: 25MB per file
6. **Add a description** (optional but helpful) — "Before - Roof, east section"
7. **Upload**

> **Tip:** Name your files before uploading. "before_roof_east.jpg" is infinitely more useful than "IMG_4721.jpg."

### Uploading Multiple Files at Once

The platform supports multi-file upload. Select all the files you want to upload at once, and the system will process them as a batch.

**Best practice:** Upload files of the same type together. Don't mix before photos with invoices — upload them as separate batches with appropriate document types.

## Photo Best Practices

### Before Photos
- **Wide shot** showing the full repair area
- **Close-up** showing the specific damage
- **Include a reference** for scale (hand, ruler, coin)
- **Same angles** you'll use for after photos
- **Label with room/area** — "Kitchen ceiling, northwest corner"

### After Photos
- **Same angles** as before photos (side-by-side comparison is powerful)
- **Well-lit** — turn on lights, open blinds, use flash
- **Clean workspace** — no tools, debris, or ladders in the shot
- **Label clearly** — "After - Roof repair, east section, 9/15/2026"

### During Photos
- **Progress milestones** — demo complete, rough-in installed, drywall hung
- **Hidden work** — things that will be covered up (wiring behind walls, subfloor under finish floor)
- **Unexpected findings** — "Opened wall, found mold on insulation"

> **Grant reporting gold:** Before/after side-by-side comparisons are the single most compelling evidence for funders. Take the time to get them right.

## Finding Documents

### In a Case

Open the case and go to the Documents tab. You can:
- **Filter by type** — show only photos, only invoices, etc.
- **Sort by date** — newest first, or oldest first
- **Search by description** — if someone labeled files well

### Across Cases

Use Case Search with document filters to find:
- All cases with missing after photos
- All cases with uploaded contracts
- All cases from a specific date range with documents

## Document Security

### Who Can See What

| Document Type | Visible To |
|---------------|-----------|
| Income verification | Assigned caseworker, Admin, Bursar |
| Photo ID | Assigned caseworker, Admin |
| Photos (before/during/after) | All assigned staff, applicant |
| Contracts | PM, Admin, applicant |
| Invoices | Bursar, PM, Admin |
| Assessments | All assigned staff |

> **Sensitive documents** (income verification, ID) have restricted access. Only people who need them for eligibility determination can view them.

### What Applicants Can See

Applicants can see:
- Photos of their own property
- Contracts they've signed
- Communication logs addressed to them

Applicants **cannot** see:
- Other applicants' documents
- Internal staff notes
- Financial records (invoices, payment records)

## Common Problems & Solutions

### "My Upload Keeps Failing"
- **Check file size** — is it over 25MB? Compress it or split it
- **Check file type** — is it a PDF, JPG, or PNG? Other formats aren't accepted
- **Check your connection** — a slow internet connection can timeout large uploads
- **Try a different browser** — some browsers handle large uploads better

### "A Photo Disappeared"
- Check the **trash/version history** — deleted files may be recoverable
- Check if someone **re-categorized** it — it may be under a different document type
- Check the **activity log** — who uploaded it, and was it later deleted?

### "I Can't See a Document I Know Exists"
- Check your **permissions** — some documents are restricted to certain roles
- Check the **case** — are you looking at the right case?
- Ask the **caseworker** — they can see what documents are attached and who can view them

## Document Retention

Different document types have different retention requirements:

| Document Type | Retention Period |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Financial records (invoices, receipts) | 7 years |
| Contracts | 10 years |
| Photos | Permanent |
| Income verification | 7 years |
| Photo ID | 3 years |
| Assessments | 5 years |

**The platform retains documents according to these schedules.** Don't manually delete documents — the system handles retention automatically.
