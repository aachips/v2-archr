### What We Ask For (And Why)

|Information|Why We Need It|
|---|---|
|**Contact Info**|To reach you about your application|
|**Home Address**|To verify location and serve your property|
|**Household Size**|To determine income eligibility|
|**Income**|To qualify for programs (no lower limit, upper limit varies)|
|**Home Ownership**|To confirm you can authorize repairs|
|**Demographics**|For grant reporting and program eligibility|
|**Repair Needs**|To assess scope of work|
|**Photos/Evidence**|To document damage and scope|

### What We DON'T Ask For

- Social Security Number
- Bank account details
- Immigration status
- Criminal history
- Medical records

### Privacy Protections

|Protection|Description|
|---|---|
|**Encryption**|All PII encrypted at rest and in transit|
|**Role-Based Access**|Only assigned staff can see your information|
|**Audit Trail**|Every access is logged|
|**No Sharing**|Information never shared outside ARCHR organizations|
|**No Selling**|We never sell data to advertisers or brokers|
|**Warrant Required**|We don't share with law enforcement without a warrant|

### What is PPI (Protected & Privileged Information)?

- All information you provide is considered private
- Protected under Privacy Act considerations
- Only used for your application and repair
- Never used for any other purpose

### Your Rights

- View your data at any time 
- Request corrections 
- Request deletion (after case closure)
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Know who has accessed your data

### The Consent Form

- You'll sign a consent form during intake
- This allows us to process your application
- You can revoke consent at any time
- If you revoke, we stop processing (but may still need to retain records)

### What Happens When Your Case is Closed

- Data retained for 7 years (grant requirements)
- Then archived to cold storage
- At 10 years, permanently deleted
- You can request early deletion (some data may need to stay for compliance)
