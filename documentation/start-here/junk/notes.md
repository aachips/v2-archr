## Section 3: Open Feedback Round (20 minutes)

### Structure

Go around the room (or around the Zoom) and give each organization 3-5 minutes to share.

Prompt:

> "I'd like to hear from each of you. A few questions to guide you:
> 
> - What's working for you in the current system?
> - What's not working?
> - If you could change one thing tomorrow, what would it be?
> 
> Please be honest. We can't fix what we don't know about."

**Reflective Listening Responses:**

|What They Say|How to Respond|
|---|---|
|"This is a mess."|"I hear you. Can you tell me more about what's specifically difficult?"|
|"I don't use it because it's too confusing."|"That's really important feedback. What would make it less confusing for you?"|
|"I'm entering data twice."|"Duplication is a huge time-waster. Where are you seeing that most?"|
|"I don't know who to talk to about problems."|"That's a failure on our end. We're going to fix that."|

### Redirection (If Needed)

|Situation|Response|
|---|---|
|Someone gets too granular|"That's helpful. Let's make a note of that and we can follow up separately."|
|Someone wants to problem-solve|"I love that idea. Let's capture it and we'll address it in the priority discussion."|
|Someone dominates the conversation|"Thank you. I'd like to hear from [next person] as well."|
