You may be asking, why are we building a version 2 of the ARCHR Platform. Chances are, if you have been using the old one to manage, track, and administrate home repairs, you are not asking that question.

The first version of the platform was built as a response to a catastrophic natural disaster. Our neighbors' home repair needs were urgent, emergent, and dire. Infrastructure built in haste is designed to break down and become obsolete. 

We wanted to build a system that is more robust and sustainable for the years and decade forward. Something that takes the model and lessons learned over a thousand home repair cases, and allows that to be shared with other groups wanting to do the same thing.

We wanted to escape the space of using Software as a Service (SaaS) to manage our unique needs. Tools used for Corporate Onboarding Portals are not fully equipped to support the needs of Regional Home Repair Coalitions. Our use of these softwares to structure our platform was creating constant issues, and pushing these tools to their limits. Updates would completely break necessary features. It was a mess.

Another intention was to make the technology that supports this work more participatory and transparent. We can do great things when we work together.
