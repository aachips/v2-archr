For Video Game Analogies, I think a helpful one would be Player Owned Ports.

In Player Owned Ports, you play the role of a Port Manager, who sends pirate and trade ships out each day. In each ship, you need a crew, which is made up of a Captain, and various crew members. These crews are made up of different levels of experience and skillsets, and each day, they are sent out, and then return at the end of the day, either successful, or having met misfortune along the way.

For each voyage, once it's sent out, it's up to that crew, the information they have, and the tools and resources they have available, to determine the success of that voyage. Once they leave, there is a limited amount we can do to support them until they get back. Almost always, the voyage will be successful, because we have great Crew Members who are experienced in what they are doing. But sometimes the unexpected happens. And we need to do our due diligence to make sure their trips are safe, that clients receive excellent service and support, and that our crews are supported and celebrated.

Something that might be fun to implement is a social media posting of that days voyage, with the opportunity for one person to volunteer buying lunch for the whole crew, as a way to support the community and a local business.
