# TASKS, LOGS & EVENTS: ONE RULE
## IF IT HAPPENED, LOG IT.
### How: Click the [+] next to "Tasks, Logs & Events"
### What to enter:
- **Which case** (start typing address or name)
- **What happened or needs to happen** (be specific)
### That's it.
---
## WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
Your entry goes to the **Unverified** queue. A Task Rabbit will review it, add details, and approve it.
---
## WHEN TO LOG:
- Need something done
- Talked to a client
- Scheduled something
- Attended an event
- Completed work
- Took photos
---
## GOOD ENTRIES ARE:
- Specific enough for someone else to understand
- Linked to a case
- Logged immediately (not later)
---
## BAD ENTRIES ARE:
- "Fix stuff"
- "Talked to client" with no name or context
- Logged a week later from memory
---
*Your logs are the organization's memory. Make them count.*
