## Who They Are

Crew Members are the people doing the hands-on repair work. They show up at job sites with their tools and materials, receive direction from the Crew Lead, and complete assigned tasks. Crew Members may be part-time, seasonal, or volunteer, and they are often not comfortable with complex software. Their primary focus is the physical work, not the paperwork.

---

## What They Do (Day to Day)

Perform Repair Tasks  
Crew Members carry out the actual repair work—roofing, plumbing, electrical, carpentry, clean-up, and whatever else the job requires.

Follow Directions  
They take direction from the Crew Lead about what to work on and when.

Attend Safety Briefings  
Every day, before work starts, Crew Members participate in a safety talk delivered by the Crew Lead. This covers the hazards of the day's work and any precautions needed.

Track Time  
They log their hours on each job—when they start, when they finish, and what they worked on.

Take Photos  
Crew Members take photos of their work, especially before and after a task is completed.

Report Issues  
If something is wrong—a safety hazard, a material issue, an unexpected problem—they flag it to the Crew Lead.

Sign Off  
If an incident occurs, Crew Members may need to sign statements.

---

## What They Need to See on Their Dashboard

Today's Jobs  
A simple list of what jobs they are assigned to, with addresses, estimated duration, and who is leading the crew.

Task List  
Once on site, a clear list of tasks for that job, with a simple way to mark tasks as complete as they go.

Safety Talk Confirmation  
A simple checkbox confirming they attended the safety talk and understood the day's hazards.

Photo Upload  
An easy way to take and upload photos of work progress, from their phone.

Hours Logging  
A simple time tracker to record when they start and finish work.

Messages  
Communications from the Crew Lead or Project Manager.

## What They Do Differently from Crew Lead

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|Task|Crew Member|Crew Lead|
|Safety Talk|Attends and signs off|Delivers the talk|
|Daily Log|Optional|Required, daily|
|Volunteer Sign-off|Logs own hours|Verifies all hours|
|Completion Documents|May witness|Gets client signature|
|Incident Reports|Signs if involved|Files them|
|Change Orders|Not involved|Requests them|
|Subcontractors|Not involved|Coordinates them|

## What Would Make Them Frustrated

- Complicated app: Crew Members aren't office workers. They need big buttons and simple actions.
    
- Slow performance: If the app lags, they won't use it.
    
- Too many steps: Marking a task complete should be one tap.
    
- No offline support: Many sites don't have internet.
    
- Can't see their assignment: If they don't know where to go, they can't work.
    
- Hours logging is hard: Time tracking needs to be trivial.
    

---

## Success Looks Like

- Crew Members know where to go and what to work on
    
- Work is completed safely and efficiently
    
- All tasks are updated in the system without extra effort
    
- Photos document the work without being a burden
    
- Hours are logged accurately
    
- The Crew Lead has everything they need to manage the site
    

---

One Rule: If you touched it, document it. A photo is worth a hundred words of notes.
