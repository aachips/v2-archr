The Envoy role is one between a Director, Representative, and Ambassador. They are an executive leadership role who provides strategic oversight over direct casework. The envoy reports to the Board and Coalition Leadership and mainly works from an office space. They need to be technically comfortable to the extent that the role requires data reporting. Each partner organization will likely have their own Envoy.

What They Need To Do:

- Approve Contracts
- Review Coalition Performance
- Set Partner Program Eligibility
- Pull summary charts and data
- Represent ARCHR to funders and community
- Review coalition budget
- Strategic planning
- Approve program changes

What they need to see:

- Coalition Overview - Total Cases, Jobs, Funding across ALL orgs
- Pending Approvals - Contracts, program changes needing sign off
- Funding Dashboard - Total Budget, spent, remaining by org
- Performance Metrics - Key indicators across the coalition
- Eligibility Configuration - Program rules, org assignments
- Org health summary - Each org’s case load, completion rate

What they need to create/update:

- Approve contracts - Review and sign-off (e-signature)
- Set eligibility rules - Configure partner program eligibility
- Pull reports - Generate summary data views
- Org Onboarding - Add/update partner organizations
- Budget Approval - Review and approve budgets
- Policy Updates - Update program guidelines
- Strategic Notes - High-level observations

Who they interact with: 

  

- Organization Admins - Program oversight, reporting
- Bursar - Financial reporting, budget
- Project Managers - Program execution
- Funders/Donors - Reporting, relationship management
- Board - Strategic Direction
- Community Leaders - Representation

  
What would frustrate them:

- Can’t see coalition-wide data - Can’t make strategic decisions / report to funders
- Too many individual case details - Need summary review
- Manual reporting - Takes too much time
- Can’t configure eligibility easily - Bottlenecks program changes
- Can’t approve contracts digitally - Slows down projects
- No funding overview - Can’t manage budget

Impact: If done well, the Envoy ensures the coalition runs smoothly, that funders trust ARCHR, that strategic decisions are informed and partners are supported. And that the Community sees leadership. If the Envoy can’t fulfill their duties, there is chaos across organizations. Funding dries up, decisions are guesses, partners leave coalition, and the community sees confusion. 

## Origin of Role

"This role came up based on a thesaurus search where the word also can mean Ambassador, Representative, or even a Prime Minister. It's not a technical role, it's not on the ground. It's like your bosses' boss."

This is a strategic leadership role - not a technical implementer, not a field worker. This person needs a helicopter view of everything happening across the coalition, not the details of any single case.

---

## Initial Assumptions to Validate

- They need coalition-wide visibility (not org-specific)
- They approve contracts but don't draft them 
- They set rules, don't process applications 
- They need summary data, not case details 
- They need to configure eligibility (approve, not implement)
- They represent ARCHR externally  
- They need quick access to reports   
- They don't need to know individual cases
