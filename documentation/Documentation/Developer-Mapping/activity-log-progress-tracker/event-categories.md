
## The Event Categories

You mentioned eight speculative categories. Here they are with what they might contain:

|Category|What It Tracks|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|**ClientEvent**|Actions related to the person/household|`client_contacted`, `client_responded`, `client_info_updated`|
|**ApplicationEvent**|Actions related to the application|`application_submitted`, `application_deduplicated`, `application_status_changed`|
|**CaseEvent**|Actions related to the case|`case_created`, `case_assigned`, `case_claimed`, `case_expired`|
|**ProjectEvent**|Actions related to the project|`project_created`, `project_budget_approved`, `project_completed`|
|**TaskEvent**|Actions related to individual tasks|`task_created`, `task_assigned`, `task_completed`|
|**RepairEvent**|Actions related to specific repairs|`repair_identified`, `repair_estimated`, `repair_completed`|
|**MilestoneEvent**|Milestone progress|`milestone_reached`, `milestone_completed`|
|**DocumentEvent**|Actions related to documents|`document_uploaded`, `document_verified`, `document_signed`|
|**CommunicationEvent**|Communications|`email_sent`, `phone_call_logged`, `message_received`|
|**ScheduleEvent**|Scheduling actions|`assessment_scheduled`, `work_started`, `deadline_approaching`|
