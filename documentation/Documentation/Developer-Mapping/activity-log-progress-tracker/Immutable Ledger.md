## The Immutable Ledger

### What "Immutable" Means

**Immutable = Cannot be changed or deleted.**

Once an entry is written to the Activity Log, it stays forever. You can't edit it. You can't delete it. You can only add new entries.

This is important because:

- You can always prove what happened and when
- Nobody can cover up mistakes or fraud
- Auditors can verify the chain of events
- You can reconstruct any case's complete history
### Why This Matters

The team member claims they did the thing. With no immutability, and no record, there is broken trust. With an immutable ledger and record, there is proof and trust is maintained. If something was changed, and we don't know who, or when, there is no accountability. If we have a log that says 'User JD at 9:14 AM', there is clear accountability.
