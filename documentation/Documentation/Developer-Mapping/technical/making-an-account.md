### Outline

**1. The Short Answer: You Don't**

- There is no public registration option
    
- Accounts are generated based on your role
    

**2. How Accounts Are Created by Role**

|Role|How Account Is Created|
|---|---|
|**Requestor (Applicant)**|At the end of intake form, you create credentials (optional but recommended)|
|**Volunteer**|Fill out volunteer interest form → account generated|
|**Staff (Caseworker, PM, etc.)**|Organization admin creates your account|
|**Subcontractor**|Project manager or admin invites you|
|**Assessor**|Organization admin creates your account|
|**Bursar**|Organization admin creates your account|
|**Admin (Organization)**|Your organization designates you|
|**Envoy**|Coalition leadership creates your account|

**3. I'm a Requestor - What Do I Do?**

- Complete the intake form (14 pages)
    
- At the end, you'll see an option to create login credentials
    
- This is optional but recommended (track your application status)
    
- Choose a username (suggested: first initial + last name)
    
- System generates a complicated password (write it down!)
    
- You can change password after first login
    

**4. I'm a Volunteer - What Do I Do?**

- Fill out the volunteer interest form
    
- You'll receive an email with your account details
    
- Login to access volunteer dashboard
    
- See available "quests" (tasks) matching your skills
    

**5. I'm Staff - What Do I Do?**

- Your admin will set up your account
    
- You'll receive an email with login details
    
- Login and set your password
    
- Your dashboard is role-specific
    

**6. Is There a Demo Account?**

- Yes! For public exploration
    
- Uses placeholder data (not real cases)
    
- Great for training and exploration
    
- Request access through the website
    

**7. I Forgot My Password**

- Use "Forgot Password" link on login page
- Reset link sent to your email
- You can also ask your organization admin
