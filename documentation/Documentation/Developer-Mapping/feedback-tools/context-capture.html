📸 Context Capture

A worksheet for capturing the moments that matter
⚡ Instead of saying "the system is broken," tell us the story of when it broke for you.
✅ Thank you! Your story has been captured. It will help us understand exactly what needs to change.
1 Which pain point does this relate to?
Select the one that best fits your story.
2 Tell the story
Describe a specific moment when this pain point happened. Be as concrete as possible.
Example:
"Last Tuesday, I was trying to find the funding status for a case in 42-CHERRY. I opened three different views, couldn't find what I needed, and ended up calling the finance team anyway. It took 20 minutes to do something that should take 2 minutes."
3 What was the impact?
How did this affect you, your work, or the people you serve?
Example:
"I wasted 20 minutes on a task that should take 2. I felt frustrated and resentful. The client had to wait an extra day for a response."
4 What should have happened?
Describe how this should work in an ideal world.
Example:
"I should have been able to open a single case view, see the funding status at the top, and know instantly whether the case was within budget. No calls needed."
5 Additional context
Optional: Any other details that might help us understand the situation.
6 Your contact info (Optional)
If you're open to follow-up questions, leave your name and email.
What happens next? Your story will be reviewed before the coalition feedback meeting. It will help us understand the real moments behind the pain points. With your permission, we may share anonymized versions to illustrate the need for specific changes.
