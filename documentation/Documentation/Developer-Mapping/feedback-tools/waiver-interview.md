# Tech Access Observation Consent Form

## What This Is

I'm observing how people interact with our home repair platform. My goal is to make the system work better for everyone - including people who use technology in different ways.

## What Will Happen

- You'll work on your normal tasks while I watch quietly
- I'll take notes about where the system feels easy or hard
- You can talk or not talk - whatever is comfortable
- If you hit a snag, I'm curious what you're thinking in that moment
- The session takes 20-30 minutes

## What I'm Looking For

- Places where the system makes sense
- Places where the system gets in your way
- Things you wish were different
- Things you've learned to work around

## Privacy & Your Choice

**You are in control.**

- You can stop at any time, for any reason
- You can skip any task you don't want to show me
- You can ask me to leave at any time

**If you choose to share anything about:**
- Medical conditions that affect how you use technology
- Disabilities (visible or invisible)
- General tech access challenges

**That information is:**
- Kept strictly private to this observation
- Only used to guide accessibility improvements
- Never shared with your manager or coworkers
- Never attributed to you in any report

You do not have to share anything you're not comfortable sharing.

## What Happens With What I Learn

- Patterns across many observations become feature improvements
- No individual will be identified in any summary or report
- The goal is better software, not evaluation of people

## Agreement

By participating, you understand:
- This is voluntary
- You can withdraw at any time
- Your job is not affected by participation or non-participation
- I am observing the SYSTEM, not evaluating YOU
