While we have a demographic of technologically literate and able clients who can fill out an online intake from a variety of devices, for the majority of the families we work with, that is not reality. Many of the households most in need of home repair assistance after a natural disaster are the ones most difficult to reach with technology.

Western North Carolina faces significant connectivity challenges [](https://www.benton.org/blog/broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina#1)[](https://www.benton.org/publications/understanding-where-and-how-support-broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina). In the 18 counties that make up our region, 84% of households have broadband of any type—meaning cellular data or wireline service—compared to 89% in the rest of the state [](https://www.benton.org/blog/broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina#1)[](https://www.benton.org/publications/understanding-where-and-how-support-broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina). The gap is even wider for reliable wireline connections: only 66% of WNC households have wireline broadband subscriptions, versus 76% for the rest of North Carolina [](https://www.benton.org/blog/broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina#1)[](https://www.benton.org/publications/understanding-where-and-how-support-broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina).

Even cellular data plans show a gap, with 77% of WNC residents having mobile data subscriptions compared to 82% elsewhere [](https://www.benton.org/blog/broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina#1)[](https://www.benton.org/publications/understanding-where-and-how-support-broadband-adoption-western-north-carolina). In Buncombe County specifically, 10% of residents have no internet access at all, and 75% of those who do have service say it is inadequate [](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/2022/03/07/fate-buncombe-broadband-lies-partially-community-survey/9346876002/#1). A full 45% of residents say internet service is too expensive to maintain [](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/2022/03/07/fate-buncombe-broadband-lies-partially-community-survey/9346876002/#1).

## The Helene Effect

Hurricane Helene didn't just damage homes and roads—it exposed how devastating the technology divide can be during a crisis. The storm destroyed fiber optic cables and power lines, leaving 95% of the telecommunications network in some WNC areas inoperable in a matter of hours [](https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/the-progress-report/crystal-clear/kayaks-used-to-reconnect-internet-after-hurricane). Internet service providers resorted to creative measures, delivering fiber cables across raging rivers via kayak to restore connectivity [](https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/the-progress-report/crystal-clear/kayaks-used-to-reconnect-internet-after-hurricane).

In the wake of the storm, 85% of community college students in the region reported being without reliable internet or technology [](https://www.ednc.org/community-college-success-coaches-helped-students-recover-from-helene-new-brief-shows/). For already underserved communities, the hurricane widened digital inequities and isolated people just when they needed connection the most [](https://dogwoodhealthtrust.org/dogwood-health-trust-announces-10-collaboratives-working-to-expand-high-speed-internet-access-and-increase-digital-skills-across-western-north-carolina/).

## How We Reach People Where They Are

**By Neighbor and Volunteer:** Much of our outreach happens through neighbors checking on neighbors. Many of our clients have not sent an email in the past five years, if ever. By equipping volunteers and community members to complete referral applications on behalf of others, we ensure that even the most isolated households can access help.

**Offline and On-the-Go:** The intake form is designed so that you can download and work on it offline, saving progress so you can submit it when you reach a place with internet coverage.

**Public Libraries:** After the storm, public libraries became essential community lifelines. They had Wi-Fi up and running fairly early on, offering a place where people could come and notify family and friends they were safe, or submit requests for emergency assistance [](https://dogwoodhealthtrust.org/partner-profile/collaboration-is-key-to-increasing-broadband-access-in-wnc/#elementor-action%3Aaction%3Dpopup%3Aopen%26settings%3DeyJpZCI6IjEyOTAwIiwidG9nZ2xlIjpmYWxzZX0%3D).

**Over the Phone:** You can always reach our office directly. If you need help completing your intake, we are available to assist over a phone call. We will fill out your responses with you, step by step.

## Our Commitment

While many features of our home repair administration and case management are automated, helping you fill out an intake will always be a human-driven process. That's our commitment.

If you are reading this, you are likely someone who can complete the intake on your own. If you want to help someone who cannot, check on your family, friends, and neighbors. Be prepared to facilitate intake submissions on their behalf. Whether you fill out the form together or call our office, we're here.
