**Our Philosophy: Assume Eligibility**

- We assume every applicant is eligible until we find disqualifying information
- This prevents eligible people from being turned away due to technicalities
- Eligibility checks are a process, not a gatekeeping moment

**2. What Makes Someone Eligible?**

|Category|Requirements|
|---|---|
|**Geography**|Property must be in service area (Buncombe, Madison, Henderson, Haywood counties)|
|**Home Ownership**|Applicant must own the home (or have legal authority to authorize repairs)|
|**Primary Residence**|Must be primary residence, not rental or vacation property|
|**Income**|No lower limit. Upper limit varies by organization (typically 80% AMI)|
|**Cause of Damage**|Damage must be related to Hurricane Helene (September 2024)|
|**Household Size**|Counts residents 50%+ of the time|
|**Demographics**|No discrimination; special programs for veterans, elderly, disabled|

**3. The Eligibility Review Process**

- **Automated Check**: System runs initial eligibility on submission
    
- **Manual Review**: Caseworker verifies documentation
    
- **Organization Match**: System finds best-fit organization (income, location, repair type)
    
- **Final Determination**: Assigned organization confirms eligibility
    

**4. What Makes Someone Ineligible (Disqualifiers)**

- Property outside service area
    
- Not a homeowner
    
- Not primary residence
    
- Previous assistance received for same repair
    
- Income exceeds all partner maximums
    
- Mobile home without lot ownership
    
- Under 18 without guardian consent
    

**5. What If I'm Denied?**

- You will receive a clear explanation
    
- You can appeal with additional documentation
    
- You may be referred to other resources
