Land of Sky runs on Office Suite. Their platform needs are mainly that they need to pull client information and client requests. To know who their clients are, where they are, and how to contact. A wish list item is for them to have a customized version of the ARCHR intake form embedded on their website. LOS has several programs offered listed on their sites [Housing Page](https://www.landofsky.org/housing.html) including and featuring <abbr title="Older Adults Home Modification Program">OAHMP</abbr> which is a federal program helping senior citizens get low cost home modifications to age safely and accessibly. These services are also offered regardless of age. Their eligibility criteria for ARCHR repairs is 62 and older, primary resident of home, family under 80% of AMI, and that they live in Buncombe, Henderson, Madison, or Transylvania County. To prove eligibility, applicants must provide a copy of drivers license, proof of income, and deed or title to home. Many applications come in mail, and they have met with clients at their home or in a public place to obtain a copy of those documents. It would be helpful if clients had options such as uploading those documents from their files, or to take a picture on their phone to send. It may be helpful to have a Release that clients sign on in the application allowing Land of Sky to share client information with partner programs. Right now the metric goal is to start slow with two home repairs a month, and scale up to 4 in a month by next January.
