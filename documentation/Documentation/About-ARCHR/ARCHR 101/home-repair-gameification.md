What if we could take the responsibility and privilege of caring for our neighbors, and turn it into a game? A game where we all can win.

Replacing dry wall or a roof can be a dreadful task. It's made much easier and joyful completing it with others. It's made fun when it's part of a collaboration between everyone.

Games can bring out the best in people, and can provide the structure for everyone to be included and involved. When making this platform, the intention was to turn a response to some of the worst devastation imaginable, into an action attraction where great things can happen.

We may not fully be there yet. But here's a vision and metaphor of a Disaster Home Repair Coalition as a video game:

+ The funding we get is our health bar. By investing in our operations, we can complete more home repairs. We can pay our partners and staff, and reduce eligibility barriers to support.
+ The players are our neighbors and community members. We all have a role in making sure we all are alright and cared for. 
+ Our Quests are our Home Repair Cases. Everyone of these quests is an adventure and opportunity to build working relationships with our neighbors we might not otherwise meet.
+ Every day we get to send out Crew Voyages to fix homes, which is the foundation for this work being done.
+ A lesser attraction of this game: In order to sustain this operation and funding support, we must maintain organized records of our cases, repairs, and where our funds go. But even that can flow like a game of Tetris.
