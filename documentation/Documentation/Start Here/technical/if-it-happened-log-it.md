# TASKS, LOGS & EVENTS: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

## Why We Log Everything

**One rule:** If it happened, log it.

Every task, every client communication, every scheduling note, every event needs to be in the system. This is how we:
- Track what's been done and what's left
- Avoid duplicate work
- Know who to contact about what
- Show funders our impact
- Protect ourselves with documentation

**If you didn't log it, it didn't happen.**

---

## When to Log

| If you... | Log it as... |
|-----------|--------------|
| Need something done | Task |
| Talk to a client on phone/email/in-person | Communication |
| Schedule an assessment or site visit | Event |
| Attend or plan a community event | Event |
| Notice something that needs attention | Task |
| Complete a repair step | Task (with update) |
| Take photos of work | Task (attach photos) |

## How to Log (Two Clicks)

### Quick Add (Anywhere, Anytime)

1. **Look for the `+` button** next to "Tasks, Logs & Events" in the sidebar
2. **Click it** → a small form opens
3. **Enter two things:**
   - Which case/project this is for (start typing the address or case number)
   - What needs to be logged (a brief description)
4. **Click Save** → you're done

**That's it.** The system captures:
- Who you are (auto-filled)
- When you logged it (auto-stamped)
- What you entered (your description)

### What If I Don't Know the Case Number?

Type the client's name or street address. The system will suggest matching cases.

### What If I Have No Internet?

**Write it down.** On paper. In a notes app. On a napkin. Then paste it into a Quick Add when you're back online.

### What If I Have 12 Things to Log?

**Log them all.** Quick Add is designed for bulk entry. Log one, click "Save & Add Another," repeat.

## The Review Process (Peer Review)

### Why Review?

Quick entries are **drafts**. They're not counted in metrics or assigned to anyone until a **Task Rabbit** reviews them.

### Who Is a Task Rabbit?

A Task Rabbit is someone (usually office-based) who:
- Has time to sit at a computer
- Knows the data fields
- Can add missing details
- Approves entries so they become real tasks

### What Task Rabbits Do

1. **Open the "Unverified" queue** (it has a badge showing how many are waiting)
2. **Review each quick entry**
3. **Add required fields:**
   - Priority (1-5)
   - Who should do this (role or person)
   - Due date
   - Subtasks (if needed)
4. **Approve or reject** the entry

### What Happens After Approval

- The entry becomes a **real task** or **log entry**
- It appears in the appropriate queue
- It gets counted in metrics
- The assigned person is notified

## Quick Log vs. Formal Entry

| Feature | Quick Log | Formal Entry (After Review) |
|---------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Who can do it | Anyone | Task Rabbits |
| Required fields | Case + Description | Priority, Assignee, Due Date, Subtasks |
| Counted in metrics | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Shows in task lists | ❌ Unverified queue only | ✅ Yes |
| Time to complete | 30 seconds | 2-5 minutes |
| When to use | In the field, on the go | At a desk, with time |

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: You're on Site, No Service

**What you do:** Take notes on your phone. "Roof: 3 missing shingles. Left side. Need ladder."

**When you get back:** Open Quick Add, paste your notes, log it.

### Scenario 2: You Just Got Off the Phone

**What you do:** Open Quick Add, select the case, type: "Call with Sarah. She confirmed assessment for Dec 18 at 2pm. She will call if she needs to reschedule."

**Why:** Next person who sees the case knows Sarah was contacted.

### Scenario 3: You're at a Desk and Have Time

**What you do:** Go to the "Unverified" queue. Look for entries without enough detail. Fill in the missing fields and approve them.

**Why:** This keeps the system accurate and helps everyone.

### Scenario 4: You Have a Lot of Tasks

**What you do:** Use Quick Add for each one. Click "Save & Add Another" after each.

**Why:** Bulk entry is faster than writing them all down somewhere else.

## Tips for Writing Good Entries

### DO:

| Good | Why |
|------|-----|
| "Schedule roof inspection - needs estimate for shingle replacement" | Clear, actionable |
| "Call with Maria - she will upload documents by Friday" | Specific, follow-up possible |
| "Need electrical quote - breaker panel is overloaded" | Identifies the issue |

### DON'T:

| Not Helpful | Why Not |
|-------------|---------|
| "Fix stuff" | What stuff? |
| "Talked to client" | Who? About what? |
| "Follow up" | When? With whom? |

> **Golden Rule:** Write enough detail that someone else could take over without having to ask you what you meant.

---

## Important Reminders

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Log it immediately** | Memory fades. Don't wait. |
| **Include the case** | Otherwise nobody knows where it goes. |
| **Be specific** | Vague logs cause confusion. |
| **Trust the process** | Quick Add → Review → Approved → Done. |
| **Someone's watching** | The ledger never forgets. Every log is permanent. |

---

## One Last Thing

> **"Did it happen?"**
>
> If yes, log it.
>
> **"Does it need to happen?"**
>
> If yes, log it as a task.
>
> **"Can I add it later?"**
>
> Yes, but add it now so you don't forget.
