### Commitment

> "We're committed to building a system that works for all of you. That means:
> 
> - Listening to your feedback
> - Communicating clearly about changes
> - Providing training and support
> - Making your work easier, not harder

Here is a quick list-explanation and criteria for good feedback: 
  
+ A specific moment in time  
+ A concrete task you were trying to do  
+ The obstacle that got in your way  
+ The outcome (what actually happened)  
+ The ideal outcome (what should have happened)
