# Volunteer Management & Hour Tracking

> **Who this is for:** Volunteers who want to help, Crew Leads who manage volunteer teams, and Admins who track volunteer contributions.

## Why It's Important

Volunteers are the lifeblood of disaster recovery. They show up, work hard, and ask for nothing but the satisfaction of helping their neighbors. **Make it easy for them.**

Volunteer hours also count as in-kind contributions for grant reporting. Tracked properly, they're worth real money to the coalition. Tracked poorly, they're invisible — and the coalition loses funding because of it.

---

## Signing Up as a Volunteer

### For New Volunteers

1. **Complete the volunteer application** — this generates your Level 0 volunteer account
2. **Set your profile** — availability, skills, experience level, any trade preferences
3. **Complete any required training** — safety orientation, platform basics
4. **Get assigned to a Crew Lead** — you'll report to someone experienced

> **Volunteer vs. Crew Member:** Volunteers are unpaid helpers supervised by a Crew Lead. Crew Members are paid staff. The platform treats them differently — volunteers see available tasks to claim; crew members see assigned tasks.

### Profile Setup

In your volunteer profile, fill out:
- **General availability** — when can you work? (weekdays, weekends, both)
- **Skills & experience** — what can you do? (demo, painting, roofing basics, etc.)
- **Physical limitations** — any restrictions we should know about? (heights, heavy lifting)
- **Emergency contact** — who do we call if something happens?
- **Dietary restrictions** — if meals are provided on-site

## Viewing and Claiming Tasks

### What You'll See

Your volunteer dashboard shows:
- **Available tasks** — volunteer-friendly tasks that need help
- **Upcoming projects** — where and when you can show up
- **Your logged hours** — running total of your contributions
- **Your certifications** — any training or clearance you've completed

### Claiming a Task

1. **Browse available tasks** — filtered to your area and skill level
2. **Click "Claim"** — this reserves a volunteer slot for that task
3. **Show up** — at the scheduled time and location
4. **Check in with the Crew Lead** — they'll confirm your assignment

> **Don't claim a task you can't complete.** If your plans change, release the slot so someone else can take it.

## Clocking In and Out

### On-Site Time Tracking

When you arrive on-site:

1. **Open the platform** on your phone or the site tablet
2. **Navigate to your assigned task**
3. **Click "Clock In"** — this starts tracking your hours
4. **Work the task** — do what the Crew Lead assigned
5. **Click "Clock Out"** when done — this stops the timer

**If you forget to clock in:**
- Tell the Crew Lead — they can manually add your hours
- Do it the same day — memories fade fast

> **GPS verification:** Some programs require GPS check-in to confirm you were on-site. This isn't about surveillance — it's about verifying volunteer hours for grant reporting.

## Logging Hours Worked

### What Gets Recorded

Each clock-in session records:
- **Date and time** — when you worked
- **Duration** — how long you worked
- **Task worked on** — which repair task
- **Location** — which property
- **Crew Lead confirmation** — they verify you were there

### Why Accurate Hours Matter

| Reason | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Grant reporting** | Funders count volunteer hours as in-kind contributions |
| **Volunteer recognition** | Milestones (50 hrs, 100 hrs, 500 hrs) get certificates |
| **Project tracking** | PMs need to know how much labor went into each task |
| **Tax documentation** | Some programs provide documentation for volunteer tax deductions |

## Reporting Volunteer Hours (For Crew Leads)

### End of Day

1. **Review the day's volunteers** — who showed up?
2. **Verify logged hours** — do the clock-in/out times look right?
3. **Add any manual entries** — volunteers who forgot to clock in
4. **Submit the daily report** — this logs all hours for the day

### End of Week

1. **Review the week's totals** — any anomalies?
2. **Recognize top contributors** — a shout-out goes a long way
3. **Plan next week's volunteer needs** — what tasks need more hands?

## Volunteer Recognition & Certification

### Milestones

The platform tracks volunteer hours and recognizes milestones:

| Milestone | Recognition |
|-----------|-------------|
| **10 hours** | Thank-you email |
| **50 hours** | Certificate of appreciation |
| **100 hours** | Recognition at coalition meeting |
| **500 hours** | Special recognition event |

### Converting from Volunteer to Crew Member

Sometimes a volunteer wants to become paid staff:

1. **Express interest** — tell your Crew Lead or Admin
2. **Complete the staff application** — separate from volunteer application
3. **Your volunteer history follows you** — all your logged hours stay on your record
4. **Your role changes** — from Level 0 volunteer to Level 1 or 2 staff

## Safety Rules for Volunteers

**Volunteers should NEVER:**
- Work at heights without proper fall protection
- Handle hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint, mold)
- Perform licensed work (electrical, plumbing, gas)
- Operate heavy machinery
- Work alone on-site

**Always:**
- Follow Crew Lead instructions
- Wear provided safety gear
- Stay in your assigned area
- Ask if you're unsure about something
- Report any injuries immediately
