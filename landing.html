Story of One — Following Eileen Bailey

A walkthrough of every screen a coalition partner sees as one household moves from intake to a finished repair, illustrating ARCHR's privacy-first defaults.

Privacy-first demo Viewed as: Jamie Davis · Caseworker · AHFH

What everyone in the coalition sees

Cases appear in shared queues with Placecodes only — no names, no street addresses, no contact details. A Placecode is the street number plus the first ten characters of the street name (e.g. 247-RIVERSIDE). It is enough to disambiguate a property without exposing the household.

Identifying information is unlocked only after a partner claims the case and the viewer's role permits it.

What the assigned caseworker sees

Once AHFH claims 247-RIVERSIDE, the assigned Caseworker (Jamie Davis) gains access to the household's identifying information for the duration of the engagement. Even here, sensitive documents stay redacted by default, contact details are gated behind explicit unlock actions, and the full address only appears alongside the work the team is currently doing.
