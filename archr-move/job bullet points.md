Software/Platform Specialist

Specialist role reporting to home repair directory. Handles development, implementation, documentation and maintenance of their intake system and online platforms, focusing primarily on user experience and interfaces (UX/UI), as well as other develoment tasks as assigned, including but not limited to - troubleshooting identified bugs, maintaining a consistent design language and collecting user feedback and feature suggestions. Specialist will collaborate with peers to serve clients. 

Essential functions:
+ update and maintain platform
+ Monitor system for errors and bugs
+ update, test, maintain automated processes -  in multiple complex and interconnected databases
- Implement and maintain front-end user interfaces.
- Promptly investigate and intervene emergent issues in platform to ensure full functionality.
- Troubleshoot authentication and log-in issues with partners
- Update System Architecture Diagrams and written Documentation to reflect updates.

Program

- Attend Operations Team meetings and document issues discussed.
- Collaborate with {manager} and individual staff members Organizations to maintain current and accurate data.
- Collaborate with Donor Relations to update dashboards, create views and generate reports as necessary for funders and community organizations.
- Maintain Application document storage in compliance with the P.I.I. & Data Sharing Policies.
- Develop and maintain website.
- Assist where necessary in other Development activities.

General

- Assist in onboarding and training new Partner Staff Members
- Maintain detailed workday logs when working off-site to ensure development continuity
  
QUALIFICATIONS:

- Commitment to the mission and values
- Commitment to racial and economic justice and experience working with diverse stakeholders.
- Ability to manage multiple complex and time-critical processes, funding requirements and relationships to complete projects on time and on budget.
- Exceptional attention to detail.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills. 
- Experience understanding and navigating complex database architecture.
- Willingness and ability to work in traumatizing and emotionally taxing environments.
- Practical experience with software development concepts including Basic Design Theory, Wireframing, User Stories and Agile Development.

Preferred

- Professional experience with Habitat for Humanity.
- Strong team orientation with excellent communication skills.
- Familiarity with Cloud Based Platforms, (Airtable, Fillout, Docupilot, Make)

WORK ENVIRONMENT AND PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

This is a hybrid-style position with work being performed as needed at the office, as well as on-location at partner Organizations Offices

Must be able to:

- Work continuously at a desk in an office setting.    
- Stand or walk for much of a workday, including working on uneven surfaces.
- See and judge distances and space relationships.
- Independently operate motor vehicles.
- Valid driver’s license, good driving record and own reliable transportation with current insurance.
