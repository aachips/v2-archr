## Carissa: The Intake Coordinator (Front Lines, Vulnerable Populations)

**What you know about her:**

- Young, warm, personable
    
- Takes calls from applicants, sometimes fills out applications for them
    
- Works with vulnerable, distrustful populations
    

**Why this matters:**  
She's the human filter. Every system flaw—confusing language, missing fields, technical glitches—lands on her shoulders first. She has to explain, soothe, and work around.

**Customized opening:**

> _"Carissa, I know you're the person on the phone, walking people through this application. Some of them are scared, some are distrustful, some just need help. I want to know what would make that easier for you—and for them."_

**Tailored questions:**

| Question                                                                                              | Why This Works                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| "What's the one question on the intake form that people ask about the most?"                          | Identifies confusing language or concepts  |
| "When you have to fill out the application for someone, what part takes the longest?"                 | Finds workflow inefficiencies              |
| "What's something an applicant has said about the process that stuck with you—good or bad?"           | Captures raw feedback from the real client |
| "If you could change one thing about how the system explains itself to applicants, what would it be?" | Focuses on user-facing language            |
| "What do you wish you knew before you started using this system that no one told you?"                | Uncovers missing training or documentation |

**A Warm-Up Question (to build trust):**

> _"You talk to people all day who are having a really hard time. How do you take care of yourself after those calls?"_

## What To Do Before Each Interview

| Step            | Time                                     | Action                                                 |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 15 min before   | Review their custom questions            | Pick 4-5 you'll actually ask                           |
| 5 min before    | Set up recording (if permission granted) | Test audio. Have backup pen and paper.                 |
| First 2 minutes | Build rapport                            | "How's your day going?" "Thanks for making time."      |
| During          | Take sparse notes                        | You want to be listening, not transcribing.            |
| Last 2 minutes  | Ask the closing question                 | "Is there anything I should have asked that I didn't?" |

## The Closing Question (Use For Everyone)

After you've asked your main questions, say:

> _"If you had a magic wand and could change one thing about this system—not a huge overhaul, just one thing—what would it be?"_

Then pause. Let them think. The best answers come after silence.
